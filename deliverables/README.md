## Abstract

**Title**: Video Game: Hurry! The Passenger

**Keywords**: Unity Game Development

**Type**: Individual Project + Team Project

**Collaborator**: [Alex Huang](https://github.com/FiveYellowMice), [Tunan Guo](https://github.com/Tup0lev), [Sylvie Chen](https://github.com/Syl-Chen)

**Duration**: July 2023 – Oct 2023

**Course Name**: [COMP3540](https://programsandcourses.anu.edu.au/2023/course/comp3540) – Game Development

**Course Outline**:

- Each student in the course was required to design an *original* game prototype. Next, students were formed into groups of four. Within each group, one of the members’ prototypes was chosen to develop into a full version game. Assessments were based on both traditional marking and peer-evaluation by different groups.

**Course Final Mark**: 88 / 100

**Project Weight**: [90%](https://programsandcourses.anu.edu.au/2023/course/COMP3540/Second%20Semester/7514) of COMP3540

**Project Mark**: 85 / 100 (prototype), 89 / 100 (full game)

**Deliverables**: [code](https://github.com/glowing-sea/hurry-the-passenger), [build](https://play.unity.com/en/games/504ab53c-0c2f-45b6-8456-bea16de5b9fc/anu2023-hurry-the-passenger)

**Description**:
- A 3D action and puzzle game based on Unity, where the player must race against time to reach the boarding gate within 15 minutes.
- A series of unique mini-games comprises the full airport journey, where players cross the road, find the check-in counter, organise luggage, pass security, and reach the boarding gate.
- Core gameplay features include: tutorials, task system, currency system, merchant NPCs, dialogue, elevators, vehicles, running, jumping, and stamina bar management.
- Core technical features include: dynamic scene loading, skybox, gravity, and momentum.

**About Collaboration**:
